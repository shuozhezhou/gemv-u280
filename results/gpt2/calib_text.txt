Last summer my family drove across the country to visit my grandparents, who live on a small farm near the river. The trip took almost a week because we stopped in many towns along the way. In the mornings we would eat breakfast at a diner, and in the afternoons my father would let us choose a museum, a park, or a lake to visit. My younger brother liked the science museum the most, especially the room with the old trains and the model of the solar system. When we finally arrived at the farm, my grandmother had cooked a large dinner with fresh corn, tomatoes, and bread from the oven. We spent the next two weeks helping with the animals, fixing the fence, and swimming in the river every evening before the sun went down. On the last night, my grandfather told us stories about how the town looked when he was a boy, when there were no paved roads and the nearest store was a full day away by horse.
