Reading: FP32 = 7.00 cycles per weight (fadd latency, II=7). INT8 = 6.99 cycles per 64-weight group
(same II=7, now per group) + ~100 cycles per row + ~37 us per call. With II=1 per group (L3) the group
term for 4096x4096 drops from 1.835 M to 0.262 M cycles, so the per-row term (~0.41 M) would dominate
unless rows are overlapped.
